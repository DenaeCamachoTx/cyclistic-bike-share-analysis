# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

## Project Overview

This capstone project analyzes 12 months of Cyclistic bike-share data from September 2025 through August 2026. The goal was to identify how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently and develop data-driven recommendations for converting casual riders into members.

## Business Question

How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

## Tools Used

* Excel and Power Query — data cleaning and validation
* BigQuery SQL — data transformation and analysis
* Python and Jupyter Notebook — analysis and visualization
* Tableau Public — interactive dashboards and station analysis
* Looker Studio — interactive reporting

## Data Preparation

The original dataset contained 6,115,982 trips. The cleaning process removed duplicate records, null values, invalid rider classifications, and trips shorter than one minute or longer than 24 hours.

* Original records: 6,115,982
* Clean records: 5,951,516
* Records removed: 164,466 (2.69%)
* Analysis period: September 2025–August 2026

## Key Findings

* Annual members accounted for 3,870,680 rides, or 65.04% of all trips.
* Casual riders accounted for 2,080,836 rides, or 34.96% of all trips.
* Casual riders averaged 18.41 minutes per trip, compared with 12.22 minutes for members.
* Members showed stronger weekday and commuting-hour usage patterns.
* Member activity peaked near 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
* Casual ridership was stronger on weekends and at recreational locations.
* Saturday was the busiest day overall, with 929,681 rides.
* Electric bikes were preferred by both rider groups.
* Navy Pier was the most popular starting and ending station, driven primarily by casual riders.

## Recommendations

1. Promote annual memberships to frequent casual riders through targeted digital campaigns.
2. Emphasize membership savings, convenience, and unlimited short-trip benefits.
3. Target advertisements around weekends, recreational locations, and high-traffic casual stations.
4. Offer trial memberships or limited-time conversion discounts during peak riding months.
5. Use location-based promotions near Navy Pier and other popular casual-rider stations.
